# Contributing to Our Project

First off, thank you for considering contributing to this project! It's people like you that make open source such a great community. We welcome contributions from everyone, whether it's fixing a bug, adding a new feature, or improving our documentation.

The following is a set of guidelines for contributing. These are mostly guidelines, not rules, but following them helps us review and merge your changes much faster.

## "Human First, Humanist" & AI Usage

We embrace modern tools, including AI, but we strongly believe in a **"Human First, Humanist"** approach to development. AI is here to assist, not to replace human understanding, empathy, and accountability.

**Crucial:** If you plan to use AI (like GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, or autonomous AI agents) to assist with your contribution, you **must** read our [AI Usage Guidelines](AI_GUIDELINES.md) before submitting a Pull Request. 

Our AI guidelines outline:
* What AI usage requires mandatory disclosure.
* Our strict requirements for providing proof of work (videos, screenshots, or detailed testing notes).
* Your accountability for any code generated by AI.
* Areas where AI usage is heavily discouraged (e.g., core architecture, UI/UX).

## How to Contribute

### 1. Reporting Bugs
If you find a bug, please check the issue tracker to see if it has already been reported. If not, open a new issue. Include as much relevant information as possible:
* A clear and descriptive title.
* Steps to reproduce the issue.
* Expected behavior vs. actual behavior.
* Environment details (OS, browser, version).

### 2. Suggesting Enhancements
We are always looking for ways to improve! If you have an idea, open an issue to discuss it before you spend time writing code. This helps ensure your idea aligns with the project's goals.

### 3. Pull Requests
When you're ready to submit code:
1. **Fork** the repository and clone it to your local machine.
2. **Create a branch** for your feature or bug fix (`git checkout -b feature/my-awesome-feature`).
3. **Write your code** and make sure it follows the project's style guidelines.
4. **Test your code** thoroughly.
5. **Commit your changes** with clear and descriptive commit messages.
6. **Push** your branch to your fork (`git push origin feature/my-awesome-feature`).
7. **Open a Pull Request** against our `main` branch.

**Note:** When opening a Pull Request, please ensure you fill out our provided **Pull Request Template**. This template includes a mandatory checklist and an AI Usage Disclosure section that you must complete.
